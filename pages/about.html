WHO WE ARE

Energy & Technology Solutions You Can Depend On

DE TSAKMA SOLAR ENERGY LTD is a renewable energy, electrical and technology solutions company focused on providing practical and dependable solutions for homes, businesses and other clients.

Our services cover solar energy systems, inverter and battery solutions, electrical installations, CCTV and security systems, networking, smart home solutions, IoT and control systems, programming, consultation and technical training.

We believe that reliable energy and practical technology can make a meaningful difference in everyday life. Our goal is to provide solutions that are properly planned, professionally installed and suited to the needs of each client.
